How we work

Four things you can count on with every job.

  • We tell you if the floor shouldn't be refinished. A floor that's too thin gets a straight answer, not a sanding you'll regret.
  • We show up when we say we will. Early, if anything, as more than one review points out.
  • We fix other people's work. Uneven installs, bad transitions, finish that peeled. It's a regular part of what we do.
  • We use professional-grade stains and finishes and we'll tell you which ones, and why, for your room.
