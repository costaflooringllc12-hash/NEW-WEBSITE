The hardwood colors homeowners want most in 2026

This year, natural wood tones are dominating interior design trends throughout New Jersey's premium residential areas.

The most requested hardwood styles include:

  • Light white oak finishes
  • Warm medium-brown hardwood
  • Matte and low-gloss finishes
  • Wide plank wood flooring

These styles help homes feel more open and timeless while matching both modern and traditional interiors. You can preview stain colors on a real floor on our floor staining page.

Many homeowners in Summit are also replacing old carpet with hardwood flooring to improve durability, simplify maintenance, and increase resale appeal.

Why hardwood flooring adds value in Westfield and Summit

Hardwood flooring continues to be one of the most valuable upgrades a homeowner can make.

Buyers consistently prefer homes with professionally installed hardwood because it offers:

  • Long-term durability
  • Easier cleaning
  • Better appearance over time
  • Higher perceived home value

That's why hardwood floor installation in Summit NJ has become one of the most requested renovation projects in the area.
