Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Costa Flooring LLC ("we," "us") collects through njcostaflooring.com and how it is used.

Information you give us

When you request an estimate, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, and details about your project. We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule an estimate, and communicate about your project.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager) that collect standard information about visits: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Cookies

Our site may set cookies for analytics and, if we run advertising, for measuring ad performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How we share information

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site and respond to requests (for example, the form-delivery and email services that forward your message to us), and only as needed for that purpose.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your estimate or project. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Data retention

We keep estimate requests and project records for as long as needed to serve you and meet our business and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing contact@njcostaflooring.com or calling (862) 452-2181.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
